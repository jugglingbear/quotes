Author: [[Martine Leavitt]]
Book: [[Calvin A Novel]]

"When I was eleven, I wondered who came up with the gross idea of taking your germy mouth--the first thing you use in the digestive process-and smashing it up against somebody else's germy mouth, which minutes before could have been masticating slimy avocados or two-month-old fruitcake. Then I turned twelve and it sounded like the most brilliant idea ever invented, as long as it was with Susie."
