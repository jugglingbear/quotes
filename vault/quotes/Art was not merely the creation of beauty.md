Author: [[Matthew Stover]]
Book: [[Blade of Tyshalle]]

"Art was not merely the creation of beauty; neither was it merely a reflection of reality. It was not even the
depiction of truth. Art was the creation of truth. It is a truism that when one is a hammer, everything looks like a
nail. The glory is art is that it can show this proverbial hammer how everything looks to a screwdriver and to a
plow share and to an earthenware pot. If reality is the sum of our perceptions, to acquire more varying points of
view is to acquire literally more reality."
