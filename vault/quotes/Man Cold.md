"It was all just supposed to be a steamy night of fun. Plague had finished its shift early and went to the bar for some drinks and met up with Leprosy. They talked and hit it off and decided to go find somewhere intimate to get to know each other better. Leprosy wanted to spice it up a bit and surprised Plague by inviting its new friend, Smallpox. Everyone had a good time and went their separate ways until... Leprosy started feeling weird in the mornings. After taking a test, it was confirmed. Their little night of fun would have consequences. Not sure what to do, Leprosy ran out of the house, dropping the test stick on the floor. As it bounced, the result indicator faced upward and clearly read the dreaded result:  
  
Man-Cold  
  
Anyway, I've got that. Not sure whether I'll live to see another sunrise. Going to stay at home to prevent starting Armageddon early. Send all the thoughts and prayers. Maybe download the app.  
  
See y'all on Monday!"
