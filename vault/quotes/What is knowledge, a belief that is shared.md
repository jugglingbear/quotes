Author: [[Orson Scott Card]]
Book: [[Hidden Empire]]

"What is knowledge? A belief that is shared by all the respectable people in a community whether there is any real
evidence for it or not.

What is faith? A belief that we hold so strongly that we act as if it is true even though we know there are many who do
not believe it.

What is opinion? A belief that we expect other people to argue with.

What is scientific fact? An oxymoron. Science does not deal in facts. It deals in hypotheses, which are never fully and
finally correct."
