Source: [[Subnautica]]

"We define organisms by their traits, but find invariably that these traits depend on those of their environment. The concept of the tadpole is meaningless without the concept of the frog it will develop into. The idea of a predator is empty without an understanding of its prey. This begs the question: If we define everything by reference to everything else, what have we actually explained?"
