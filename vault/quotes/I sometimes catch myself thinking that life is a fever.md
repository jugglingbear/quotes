Author: [[Matthew Stover]]
Book: [[Blade of Tyshalle]]

"I sometimes catch myself thinking that life is a fever. That the universe fell ill two or three billion years
ago and life, in all its fantastic improbability, is the universe's fever dream. That the harsh intractability
of the inanimate is the immune system of reality attempting to cure it of life. That when life is extinguished,
the universe will awaken, yawn and stretch and shake its metaphoric head at its bizarre imagination to have
produced such an unlikely dream."
