Author: [[Matthew Stover]]
Book: [[Blade of Tyshalle]]

"Most people cannot face a world without rules. Those rules of life and society—rules that are so often railed
against as stifling and degrading—serve a profound purpose: they provide patterns of behavior that allow one the
comfort of believing one understands the rules of the game. Without rules, there is no game."
