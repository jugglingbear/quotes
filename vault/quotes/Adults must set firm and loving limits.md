Author: [[Foster Cline]]
Book: [[Parenting with Love and Logic]]

"Adults must set firm, loving limits using enforceable statements without showing anger, lecturing, or using threats.
The statements are enforceable because they deal with how we will respond. When a child causes a problem, the adult
shows empathy through sadness and sorrow and then lovingly hands the problem and its consequences back to the child."
