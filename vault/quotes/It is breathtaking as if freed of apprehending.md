Author: [[Brent Weeks]]
Book: [[Night Angel: Nemesis]]

"It is breathtaking, as if, freed of apprehending a woman solely through the tiny aperture of my libido, I stand suddenly at a hilltop, seeing the rising sun of her in her full glory, as she takes on depth of color I'd never noticed before, whole horizons unfolding: not woman merely as a potential lover, but as friend, as confidante, as potential soul mate, as someone's daughter, a sister, a mother. A woman whole, enmeshed in the rich weave of her relationships, of which the least is some few flirtations with me."
