Author: [[Brandon Sanderson]]
Book: [[Tress of the Emerald Sea]]]]

"We each make our own lore, our own legends, every day. Our memories are our ballads, and if we tweak them a little with each performance... well, that's all in the name of good drama. The past is boring anyway. We always pretend the ideals and culture of the past have aged like wine, but in truth, the ideas of the past tend to age more like biscuits. They simply get stale."
