Author: [[Norton Juster]]

"One of the nicest things about mathematics, or anything else you might care to learn, is that many of the things which can never be, often are. It's very much like trying to reach Infinity. You know that it's there but you just don't know where--but just because you can never reach it doesn't mean it's not worth looking for."
