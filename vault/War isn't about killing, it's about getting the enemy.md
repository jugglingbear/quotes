Author: [[Orson Scott Card]]
Book: [[Empire]]

"War isn't about killing; it's about getting the enemy to stop resisting your will. Like training a dog not to bite. Punishing him leaves you with a beaten dog. Killing him is a permanent solution but now you've got no dog. If you can understand why he's biting and remove the conditions that make him bite, sometimes that can resolve the problem as well. The dog isn't dead. He isn't even your enemy."
