Author: [[Scott Meyer]]
Book: [[Off to be the Wizard]]

"Martin was done worrying about the philosophical implications of his discovery. He had finally come to see it like this: Some say the universe was created by God and we are powerless pawns to His whim. Some say the universe was created by random chance and we are powerless specs in a vast, indifferent ocean. Martin could prove that the world was created by a computer program, which made no difference because--who created the program? God? Random chance? He hadn't answered the question. He had just pushed it back one step."
